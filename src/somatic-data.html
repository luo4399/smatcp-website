Somatic Mosaicism Data Portal

We provide raw PCR-free bulk whole-genome sequencing (WGS) data from 354 samples spanning 30 normal tissue types, PacBio HiFi WGS data of whole-blood samples from 68 donors, and RNA-seq data from 1,613 samples across 33 tissue types, comprising a total of 7,249 files and 623.8 TB of data. Together, these datasets offer a multidimensional resource for characterizing somatic mosaicism, germline variations, tissue-specific transcription, and their relationships across individuals and tissues. The data are hosted in a secure, access-controlled cloud environment (https://cloud.smart-nbc.org.cn/) that supports dataset discovery and approved research use while safeguarding participant privacy.
